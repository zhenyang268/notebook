## 短文

### 短文
In public debate, an argument is often judged by its conclusion rather than by the quality of its reasoning. A speaker may make a confident claim, yet the claim gains force only when it rests on a sound premise and adequate evidence. If the premise is weak or the evidence selective, the conclusion, however attractive, remains unsupported. Equally important is the assumption that links the premise to the conclusion; once that hidden assumption is exposed, the whole argument may collapse. A strong justification therefore requires more than repetition: it requires a counterargument to be acknowledged and answered. The implication is clear: readers should examine not only what is asserted but also how the assertion is supported. Otherwise, they may mistake rhetorical confidence for proof and accept a conclusion that argument does not warrant.

### 中译
在公共讨论中，一个论证往往依据其结论来评判，而不是依据其推理的质量。发言者可能作出一个自信的主张，但该主张只有在建立在可靠前提和充分证据之上时才有力量。如果前提薄弱或证据具有选择性，那么结论无论多么吸引人，仍然缺乏支持。同样重要的是把前提与结论连接起来的假设；一旦这一隐含假设被揭示，整个论证就可能崩塌。因此，有力的辩护需要的不是重复，而是承认并回应反论点。其含义很清楚：读者不仅应检查被断言了什么，还应检查该断言是如何得到支持的。否则，他们可能把修辞上的自信误当作证明，并接受一个论证并不支持的结论。

### 语法点
1. `an argument is often judged by its conclusion rather than by the quality of its reasoning` → 被动语态 + `rather than` 并列介词短语 → 用于客观陈述评价标准；`rather than` 前后应保持结构对称。
2. `the claim gains force only when it rests on a sound premise and adequate evidence` → 主句 + `only when` 引导条件状语从句 → 用于强调必要条件；`only when` 置于句首时主句需部分倒装，此处后置故不倒装。
3. `If the premise is weak or the evidence selective, the conclusion, however attractive, remains unsupported.` → `if` 条件状语从句 + `however + 形容词` 插入让步 + 主句 → 用于先立条件再作让步；`however attractive` 相当于 `no matter how attractive`。
4. `Equally important is the assumption that links the premise to the conclusion` → 表语前置完全倒装 + `that` 引导定语从句 → 用于强调重要性并引出核心名词；`that` 在定语从句中作主语。
5. `readers should examine not only what is asserted but also how the assertion is supported` → `not only...but also` 并列两个名词性从句 + 被动语态 → 用于并列两个考察对象；`what` 引导被断言的内容，`how` 引导支持方式。
