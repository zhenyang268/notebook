## 短文

### 短文
Over the past decade, the proportion of urban households owning electric cars has risen steadily, while the percentage relying on public transport has declined. Although total car sales increased substantially in 2023, growth was marginal in smaller cities, where demand continued to fluctuate with fuel prices. Nationally, electric vehicles accounted for nearly one third of new purchases, a figure that exceeds earlier forecasts. The overall trend is clear: conventional models are losing ground, but their sales have not yet fallen below those of hybrids. Industry analysts expect the share of electric cars to peak around 2030 and then remain stable. Even so, charging infrastructure must increase at a similar pace; otherwise, the upward trend could slow. Policymakers should therefore monitor these figures closely and adjust subsidies when necessary.

### 中译
过去十年，拥有电动汽车的城市家庭比例稳步上升，而依赖公共交通的比例则有所下降。尽管2023年汽车总销量大幅增长，但小城市的增长幅度很小，那里的需求继续随燃油价格波动。在全国范围内，电动汽车占新购车辆近三分之一，这一数字超过了此前的预测。总体趋势很明确：传统车型正在失去优势，但其销量尚未低于混合动力车型。行业分析师预计，电动汽车的份额将在2030年前后达到峰值，然后保持稳定。即便如此，充电基础设施也必须以类似速度增长；否则，上升趋势可能会放缓。因此，政策制定者应密切关注这些数字，并在必要时调整补贴。

### 语法点
1. `the proportion of urban households owning electric cars has risen steadily` → 现在完成时（has risen）+ 程度副词（steadily） → 用来描述从过去延续到现在的数量变化；`owning...` 为现在分词后置定语。
2. `while the percentage relying on public transport has declined` → while 引导对比从句；`relying on...` 为现在分词后置定语 → 用来对比两个同时发生的趋势，百分比作主语。
3. `Although total car sales increased substantially in 2023, growth was marginal in smaller cities` → although 引导让步状语从句 → 先承认总体增长，再转折强调局部增长幅度很小。
4. `smaller cities, where demand continued to fluctuate with fuel prices` → where 引导非限制性定语从句 → 补充说明地点或范围背景；`fluctuate with` 表示“随……波动”。
5. `a figure that exceeds earlier forecasts` → 同位语 `a figure` + that 引导定语从句 → 用 `a figure` 概括前文数字，再由 that 从句补充比较；that 在从句中作主语。
