## 短文

### 短文
In an age when information spreads faster than ever, students must learn to perceive the difference between evidence and opinion. Those who merely memorize facts often fail to distinguish a sound claim from a misleading one. What matters is not how much information one acquires, but whether one can comprehend its meaning and evaluate its reliability. Instead of accepting what they read, learners should investigate the sources, analyze the methods behind a study, and verify key data before drawing conclusions. Only when they compare competing explanations can they infer which one is better supported. If evidence remains weak, they should assume uncertainty rather than rush to judgment. Such habits do not weaken learning; they make it more disciplined and more honest.

### 中译
在信息传播速度空前之快的时代，学生必须学会辨别证据与观点。只记事实的人往往无法区分可靠的论断与误导性的论断。重要的不是一个人获取了多少信息，而是他能否理解其含义并评估其可靠性。学习者不应被动接受所读内容，而应调查信息来源、分析研究背后的方法，并在得出结论前核实关键数据。只有当他们比较相互竞争的解释时，才能推断出哪一种解释得到更有力的支持。如果证据仍然薄弱，他们应假定存在不确定性，而不是急于下判断。这样的习惯不会削弱学习；反而会使学习更有章法、更诚实。

### 语法点
1. “Those who merely memorize facts often fail to distinguish a sound claim from a misleading one.” → who 引导定语从句 + fail to do + distinguish A from B → 先用定语从句限定“哪些人”，再用 fail to do 说明结果，用 distinguish A from B 表“区分 A 与 B”。
2. “What matters is not how much information one acquires, but whether one can comprehend its meaning and evaluate its reliability.” → What 引导主语从句 + not...but... 连接两个表语从句 → 用 What matters is... 引出真正重点，用 not...but... 形成对比。
3. “Only when they compare competing explanations can they infer which one is better supported.” → Only + 状语从句置句首引起部分倒装 + which 引导宾语从句 → 强调条件时主句用“can they infer”倒装；infer 后接宾语从句。
4. “Instead of accepting what they read, learners should investigate the sources, analyze the methods behind a study, and verify key data before drawing conclusions.” → Instead of + 动名词 + what 宾语从句 + should + 并列动词原形 + before + 动名词 → 用 Instead of 表替代，三个谓语并列，before drawing 表时间先后。
5. “If evidence remains weak, they should assume uncertainty rather than rush to judgment.” → If 条件状语从句 + should + rather than 连接并列动词原形 → 条件句表假设，rather than 表取舍，前后动词形式保持一致。
