## 短文

### 短文
Students who wish to write persuasively should pay attention to adverbs and prepositional phrases, which are often overlooked. By and large, these small expressions do not attract attention; in particular, phrases such as “to some extent” or “in the long run” can, however, shape a writer’s tone considerably. If learners deliberately collect such items and readily put them into practice, their prose will eventually become more precise. Simultaneously, they must avoid using them at random, for a phrase chosen in vain may confuse rather than clarify. On the whole, mastery does not arise overnight; it depends, to a large extent, on repeated exposure and careful revision. In this sense, what seems merely decorative can, in the long run, determine whether an argument is persuasive.

### 中译
希望写出有说服力文章的学生应当关注副词和介词短语，而它们常常被忽视。总的来说，这些小型表达并不会引人注意；尤其是“在某种程度上”或“从长远来看”这类短语，却能在相当程度上塑造作者的语调。如果学习者有意收集这类表达并乐于将其付诸实践，他们的文笔最终会变得更加精准。同时，他们必须避免随意使用这些表达，因为一个徒劳选用的短语可能使人困惑而非澄清。总体而言，掌握并非一夜之间就能实现；它在很大程度上依赖于反复接触和仔细修改。从这个意义上说，看似只是装饰性的表达，从长远来看，可能决定一个论点是否具有说服力。

### 语法点
1. “Students who wish to write persuasively should pay attention to...” → who引导限制性定语从句，修饰Students；主句谓语为should pay attention to → 用于说明哪类学生应当关注某事，定语从句紧跟先行词，情态动词should表达建议。
2. “By and large, these small expressions do not attract attention” → By and large为句首评注性状语；do not构成一般现在时否定 → 用于整体评价某一现象，评注状语后用逗号隔开，否定句借助助动词do。
3. “If learners deliberately collect such items and readily put them into practice, their prose will eventually become more precise.” → if引导条件状语从句，从句中含并列谓语collect and put；主句用will+动词原形 → 条件从句常用一般现在时表将来，主句用将来时；deliberately、readily分别修饰谓语动词。
4. “a phrase chosen in vain may confuse rather than clarify” → chosen in vain为过去分词短语作后置定语；rather than连接并列动词confuse和clarify → 过去分词短语修饰phrase，表示被动/完成意义；rather than连接两个并列成分，表示“而不是”。
5. “what seems merely decorative can, in the long run, determine whether an argument is persuasive.” → what引导主语从句；in the long run为插入性状语；whether引导宾语从句作determine的宾语 → what从句整体作主语，谓语为can determine；插入状语不影响句子结构，whether从句说明“决定是否……”。
