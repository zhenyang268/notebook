## 短文

### 短文
Research in the social sciences rarely proceeds by intuition alone. A scholar usually begins with a hypothesis derived from the existing literature and then designs a methodology capable of testing it. In quantitative studies, researchers control variables and rely on empirical data to validate their claims; in qualitative work, by contrast, interviews and observation may reveal how a framework operates in practice. Yet no single study can settle a question. Before findings enter the academic record, they must survive peer review, and even then independent teams are expected to replicate the results. This process is slow, but it protects knowledge from being confused with opinion. For students, the lesson is clear: to read critically is to ask not only what a study concludes, but how it reached that conclusion and whether its evidence is strong enough to support it.

### 中译
社会科学研究很少仅凭直觉推进。学者通常从现有文献中提出一个假设，然后设计一种能够检验该假设的方法。在定量研究中，研究者控制变量并依赖实证数据来验证其主张；相比之下，在定性研究中，访谈和观察可能揭示某一框架在实践中如何运作。然而，任何单一研究都无法解决一个问题。在研究结果进入学术记录之前，它们必须经受同行评审；即便那时，独立团队也应重复这些结果。这一过程缓慢，但它防止知识被混同于意见。对学生而言，教训很清楚：批判性阅读不仅要问一项研究得出了什么结论，还要问它如何得出该结论，以及其证据是否足以支持该结论。

### 语法点
- `A scholar usually begins with a hypothesis derived from the existing literature` → 过去分词短语作后置定语 → `derived from...` 修饰 `hypothesis`，相当于 `which is derived from...`；学术写作中常用于压缩定语从句，表被动或来源。
- `a methodology capable of testing it` → 形容词短语作后置定语 → `capable of doing` 修饰 `methodology`；`capable of + 动名词` 说明名词的能力或用途。
- `may reveal how a framework operates in practice` → `how` 引导宾语从句 → 作 `reveal` 的宾语，从句用陈述语序；用于说明方式或运作机制。
- `Before findings enter the academic record, they must survive peer review` → `Before` 引导时间状语从句 + 主句情态动词 → `before` 从句用一般现在时表将来，主句 `must` 表必要性；`findings` 复数作主语，`enter` 用原形。
- `not only what a study concludes, but how it reached that conclusion and whether its evidence is strong enough to support it` → `not only...but...` 并列名词性从句 → `what/how/whether` 三个宾语从句并列作 `ask` 的宾语；从句均用陈述语序，`whether` 引导一般疑问意义。
