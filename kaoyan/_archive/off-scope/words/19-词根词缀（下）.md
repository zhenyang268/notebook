## 短文

### 短文

Anyone who has studied English vocabulary seriously soon discovers that roots and affixes are not merely a list to be memorised. Take the root *spect*, meaning “to look”. Once you know it, you can inspect an unfamiliar word, predict its meaning from its parts, and describe it accurately to others. Similarly, *port*, “to carry”, explains why a portable device is easy to carry and why a transfer of funds moves money from one account to another. The same logic applies to *dict* (to say), *duct* (to lead) and *pos* (to place): it is this underlying system that makes a word like *compose* or *conduct* transparent rather than arbitrary. Learners who master a few dozen such elements therefore convert passive recognition into active control, and they submit new words to analysis instead of merely memorising them. The gain is visible within weeks.

### 中译

任何认真学过英语词汇的人都会很快发现，词根与词缀并不只是一份用来背诵的清单。以词根 *spect*（意为“看”）为例。一旦你掌握了它，你就能审视一个陌生的单词，依据其组成部分预测它的含义，并向他人准确地描述它。同样，*port*（“携带”）可以解释为什么便携设备易于携带，也可以解释为什么资金转账会把钱从一个账户转移到另一个账户。同样的逻辑也适用于 *dict*（说）、*duct*（引导）和 *pos*（放置）：正是这一底层系统，使得 *compose* 或 *conduct* 这样的词变得透明可解，而不是任意武断。因此，掌握了数十个这类要素的学习者，就把被动的辨认转化为主动的掌控；他们让新词接受分析，而不是仅仅死记硬背。这种收益在几周之内就清晰可见。

### 语法点

1. `Anyone who has studied English vocabulary seriously soon discovers that roots and affixes are not merely a list to be memorised.` → 定语从句（who…）嵌套宾语从句（that…），宾语从句中又含不定式被动式作后置定语（to be memorised） → 写议论文开头下判断时，可用“Anyone who… discovers that…”把人、事、结论三层一次说清，避免短句堆叠。

2. `Take the root spect, meaning “to look”.` → 祈使句 + 现在分词短语作后置修饰（= which means） → 举例时用祈使句引入，再以分词短语补充释义，比另起一句更紧凑；分词逻辑主语须与所修饰名词一致。

3. `it is this underlying system that makes a word like compose or conduct transparent rather than arbitrary.` → It is … that … 强调句型，强调主语 this underlying system → 阅读中见 it is/was … that … 且去掉后句子仍完整，即为强调句；写作中用来突出因果链中的关键因素。

4. `Once you know it, you can inspect an unfamiliar word, predict its meaning from its parts, and describe it accurately to others.` → once 引导时间状语从句 + 情态动词并列谓语（inspect / predict / describe） → once 相当于“一旦”，从句用一般现在时表将来；三个动词并列时只保留第一个情态动词，后两个用原形，句子更简洁。

5. `Learners who master a few dozen such elements therefore convert passive recognition into active control, and they submit new words to analysis instead of merely memorising them.` → 定语从句 + convert … into … 固定搭配；“instead of + 动名词”作状语表取舍 → instead of 后须接名词或动名词，不能接不定式；用于对比“正确做法与错误做法”时，可使论证立场鲜明。
