## 语法短文

**短文**

People often describe emotion as noise that disturbs clear thinking. Research on cognition suggests a different view: emotion is part of the system that decides what deserves attention. When a person feels threatened, attention narrows; when they feel curious, it widens. Such changes are not merely subjective. They influence what is encoded in memory and how quickly a choice is made under pressure. It is therefore misleading to train reason by suppressing feeling. Only when learners recognize their emotional state in real time can they judge whether it is helping or distorting their thinking. This does not mean that every feeling should be trusted. Rather, emotional awareness should be treated as a skill that can be practiced, not as a weakness to be hidden.

**译文**

1. People often describe emotion as noise that disturbs clear thinking. → 人们常把情绪说成干扰清晰思考的噪音。
2. Research on cognition suggests a different view: emotion is part of the system that decides what deserves attention. → 认知研究提出另一种看法：情绪是决定什么值得注意的系统的一部分。
3. When a person feels threatened, attention narrows; when they feel curious, it widens. → 当一个人感到受威胁时，注意会收窄；当他们感到好奇时，注意会变宽。
4. Such changes are not merely subjective. → 这类变化并不只是主观的。
5. They influence what is encoded in memory and how quickly a choice is made under pressure. → 它们影响什么被编码进记忆，也影响在压力下作出选择的速度。
6. It is therefore misleading to train reason by suppressing feeling. → 因此，靠压抑感受来训练理性是误导性的。
7. Only when learners recognize their emotional state in real time can they judge whether it is helping or distorting their thinking. → 只有当学习者实时识别自己的情绪状态时，他们才能判断这种状态是在帮助还是在扭曲思考。
8. This does not mean that every feeling should be trusted. → 这并不意味着每一种感受都应被信任。
9. Rather, emotional awareness should be treated as a skill that can be practiced, not as a weakness to be hidden. → 相反，情绪觉察应被视为一种可以练习的技能，而不是一种需要隐藏的弱点。

**语法点**

- *that disturbs clear thinking* → 定语从句 → 修饰 noise，说明情绪被描述成哪一种“噪音”。
- *what deserves attention* → 名词性从句（宾语从句） → 作 decides 的宾语，说明系统所决定的内容。
- *to train reason by suppressing feeling* → 非谓语（不定式短语作真正主语；by suppressing 为介词加动名词） → 在 It is ... 结构中充当真正主语，表达被评价的行为。
- *Only when learners recognize their emotional state in real time can they judge* → 部分倒装（Only + 状语从句置于句首） → 强调条件限制，并使主句助动词 can 提前。
- *should be treated* → 被动语态（情态动词 + be + 过去分词） → 说明情绪觉察应被如何对待，突出规约性建议。
