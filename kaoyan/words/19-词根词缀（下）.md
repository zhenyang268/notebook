## 语法短文

Word roots and affixes are often advertised as a shortcut to vocabulary, yet they work best as a tool for informed guessing rather than as a substitute for context. A single root may change its form and meaning across languages: the element in "transfer" looks different in "fertile," and a prefix such as "in-" can mean either "not" or "into." Learners who treat roots as fixed codes therefore misread unfamiliar words. A more reliable method is to split a long word into recognizable parts, check the possible meaning against the sentence, and confirm it in a dictionary. Only when a guessed meaning is tested in context does it become useful. Used this way, roots reduce the burden of memorization and make new words easier to retain. What matters is not how many roots you can list, but whether you can apply them carefully.

**译文**
1. Word roots and affixes are often advertised as a shortcut to vocabulary, yet they work best as a tool for informed guessing rather than as a substitute for context. → 词根和词缀常被宣传为通往词汇的捷径，但它们最好的用途是帮助有依据地猜测，而不是替代语境。
2. A single root may change its form and meaning across languages: the element in "transfer" looks different in "fertile," and a prefix such as "in-" can mean either "not" or "into." → 同一个词根在不同语言中可能改变形式和意义：出现在“transfer”中的成分在“fertile”中看起来不同，而像“in-”这样的前缀既可表示“不”，也可表示“进入”。
3. Learners who treat roots as fixed codes therefore misread unfamiliar words. → 因此，把词根当作固定编码的学习者会误读不熟悉的单词。
4. A more reliable method is to split a long word into recognizable parts, check the possible meaning against the sentence, and confirm it in a dictionary. → 更可靠的方法是：把长单词拆成可识别的部分，结合句子检验可能的词义，并在词典中确认。
5. Only when a guessed meaning is tested in context does it become useful. → 只有当猜测出的词义在语境中经过检验，它才会变得有用。
6. Used this way, roots reduce the burden of memorization and make new words easier to retain. → 这样使用时，词根能减轻记忆负担，并使新词更容易记住。
7. What matters is not how many roots you can list, but whether you can apply them carefully. → 重要的不是你能列出多少词根，而是你是否能谨慎地运用它们。

**语法点**
- *Learners who treat roots as fixed codes* → 定语从句（who引导） → 修饰先行词Learners，限定“把词根当作固定编码”的这类学习者，使后文“误读生词”的判断有明确对象，避免泛泛而谈。
- *Used this way* → 过去分词短语作方式或条件状语 → 逻辑主语是主句的roots，相当于“如果以这种方式使用”，把前文的方法与后文的结果连接起来，同时避免重复主语，使句子紧凑。
- *Only when a guessed meaning is tested in context does it become useful.* → Only加状语从句置于句首引起主句部分倒装；其中is tested为被动语态 → 倒装强调“在语境中检验”是词义变得有用的必要条件；被动使“被猜测的词义”成为被检验对象，突出动作承受者。
- *What matters is not how many roots you can list, but whether you can apply them carefully.* → 名词性从句（What matters为主语从句，how many...与whether...并列作表语从句） → 用主语从句提出核心问题，再用not...but...对比两个表语从句，突出“能否运用”而非“能列多少”，使论点落在实际能力上。
- *they work best as a tool for informed guessing rather than as a substitute for context* → rather than连接的并列介词短语（as...rather than as...） → 对比“有依据猜测的工具”和“语境的替代品”两种定位，直接支撑“词根词缀不能替代语境”的中心论点。
