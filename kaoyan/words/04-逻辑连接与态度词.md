## 语法短文

Many students believe that a good argument consists of piling up evidence. However, it is concession, not accumulation, that makes writing persuasive. Although a writer may fear that admitting weaknesses will undermine his position, the opposite is usually true. What convinces a reader is the sense that the author has weighed counterarguments fairly. Therefore, connectives such as “although”, “nevertheless” and “on the contrary” should not be treated as decoration; they signal the writer's attitude towards his own claims. Had the writer ignored opposing views entirely, the reader would have suspected either dishonesty or ignorance. Being aware of this, skilled writers deliberately concede a small point before attacking a larger one. Consequently, the argument appears balanced, and the conclusion, though modest, is far more likely to be accepted.

**译文**

1. Many students believe that a good argument consists of piling up evidence.
   许多学生认为，一篇好的论证就是把证据堆砌起来。

2. However, it is concession, not accumulation, that makes writing persuasive.
   然而，真正让文章有说服力的是让步，而不是累积。

3. Although a writer may fear that admitting weaknesses will undermine his position, the opposite is usually true.
   尽管作者可能担心承认弱点会削弱自己的立场，事实通常恰恰相反。

4. What convinces a reader is the sense that the author has weighed counterarguments fairly.
   能让读者信服的，是那种作者已公正权衡过反方意见的感觉。

5. Therefore, connectives such as “although”, “nevertheless” and “on the contrary” should not be treated as decoration; they signal the writer's attitude towards his own claims.
   因此，“although”“nevertheless”“on the contrary”这类连接词不应被当作装饰；它们标示出作者对自己论断的态度。

6. Had the writer ignored opposing views entirely, the reader would have suspected either dishonesty or ignorance.
   倘若作者完全无视对立观点，读者便会怀疑他要么不诚实，要么见识不足。

7. Being aware of this, skilled writers deliberately concede a small point before attacking a larger one.
   意识到这一点，娴熟的作者会刻意先让出一小步，再攻击更大的一点。

8. Consequently, the argument appears balanced, and the conclusion, though modest, is far more likely to be accepted.
   于是，论证显得持平，而结论虽然温和，却远更可能被接受。

**语法点**

1. *it is concession, not accumulation, that makes writing persuasive* → it 强调句（分裂句）→ 把「让步」这一与「堆砌」相对立的因素置于焦点位置，使对比成为全段的论点核心，而非一句普通的陈述。

2. *Although a writer may fear that admitting weaknesses will undermine his position* → 让步状语从句（内含 that 引导的宾语从句，动名词短语 admitting weaknesses 作从句主语）→ 先替读者说出顾虑，为后半句 the opposite is usually true 的转折搭好台阶，让步因此成为论证手段而非语气软化。

3. *What convinces a reader is the sense that the author has weighed counterarguments fairly* → what 引导的主语从句 + that 引导的同位语从句 → 主语从句把「说服力的来源」提到句首充当话题，同位语从句则具体交代 sense 的内容，避免句子头重脚轻。

4. *Had the writer ignored opposing views entirely, the reader would have suspected either dishonesty or ignorance* → 省略 if 的条件句倒装（虚拟语气，与过去事实相反）→ 以反证方式说明「完全不提反方」的后果，比直陈句更有力度，同时省去 if 使行文更紧凑、更书面。

5. *Being aware of this, skilled writers deliberately concede a small point before attacking a larger one* → 现在分词短语作状语（非谓语动词）→ 表原因，说明娴熟作者为何先作让步；若改用原因状语从句，句子会冗长，且与上一句的论证节奏重复。
