## 短文

### 短文
Admittedly, logical connectives may seem merely decorative to students who equate good writing with long words. Nevertheless, they are the hinges on which an argument turns. A writer who says “therefore” claims that a conclusion follows, whereas one who says “in contrast” signals that two cases diverge; moreover, such words tell readers how to weigh evidence. Consequently, a passage, albeit grammatically flawless, can remain logically fragile. Arguably, the most demanding task in reading is not translating every sentence but detecting the attitude behind it. Presumably, examiners choose texts in which such signals carry an argumentative burden; conversely, careless readers may ignore them. Notwithstanding their apparent simplicity, these connectives shape interpretation. Mastering them makes reading more reliable and therefore less anxious.

### 中译
诚然，对于把好文章等同于长词的学生来说，逻辑连接词似乎只是装饰。然而，它们是论点赖以转动的铰链。说“therefore”的作者声称某个结论随之成立，而说“in contrast”的作者则表明两种情况存在分歧；此外，这类词告诉读者如何权衡证据。因此，一段文字尽管在语法上无可挑剔，仍可能在逻辑上脆弱。可以说，阅读中最艰巨的任务不是翻译每一个句子，而是察觉其背后的态度。想必，出题人会选择那些由这类信号承载论证负担的文本；相反，粗心的读者可能忽略它们。尽管这些连接词看似简单，它们却塑造着理解。掌握它们会使阅读更可靠，因而更少焦虑。

### 语法点
1. `may seem merely decorative to students who equate good writing with long words` → 情态动词 may + 系动词 seem + 形容词补语，后接 who 定语从句 → 用于让步后委婉判断，避免绝对化；who 从句限定 students。
2. `the hinges on which an argument turns` → 介词 on + 关系代词 which 引导的定语从句 → 正式书面语中把介词提前，使衔接更紧凑；先行词为 hinges。
3. `A writer who says “therefore” claims that..., whereas one who says “in contrast” signals that...` → whereas 引导对比并列复句，前后结构对应，one 替代 a writer → 用于转折或对比，注意前后句法平衡。
4. `a passage, albeit grammatically flawless, can remain logically fragile` → albeit 引导让步状语作插入语，后接形容词短语；主句为 can remain + 形容词 → 表示“尽管……仍……”，书面正式。
5. `Notwithstanding their apparent simplicity, these connectives shape interpretation.` → notwithstanding + 名词短语作让步状语，置于句首 → 相当于 despite，语气正式；主句用一般现在时陈述普遍判断。
