## 短文

### 短文
In academic writing, precise vocabulary is crucial. Replacing vague words such as "good" or "bad" with more exact expressions is not merely a stylistic preference but an indispensable step toward clear argumentation. A well-chosen word can be beneficial, making a claim convincing; a careless one may prove detrimental, weakening the writer's credibility. This is especially true when students discuss social issues, where adverse consequences and significant differences often require careful distinction. The effect of precise diction is profound: it enables writers to present constructive proposals rather than empty complaints, and it gives them an advantageous position in academic communication. Therefore, learners should treat vocabulary expansion as a desirable habit, revise every sentence with care, and avoid the temptation to rely on convenient but imprecise words.

### 中译
在学术写作中，精准的词汇至关重要。用更准确的表达替换“good”或“bad”这类模糊词，不仅仅是一种文体偏好，而是通向清晰论证不可或缺的一步。一个精心选用的词可能大有裨益，使论点令人信服；一个草率的词则可能有害，削弱作者的可信度。当学生讨论社会问题时尤其如此，因为不良后果和显著差异往往需要仔细区分。精准用词的效果是深远的：它使作者能够提出建设性的建议而非空洞的抱怨，并使他们在学术交流中处于有利位置。因此，学习者应把扩大词汇量视为一种可取的习惯，认真修改每一个句子，并避免依赖方便却不精确词语的诱惑。

### 语法点
- `is not merely a stylistic preference but an indispensable step` → `not merely...but...` 并列递进结构 → 用于强调“不只是A，而是B”，适合在作文中提升论证层次。
- `A well-chosen word can be beneficial, making a claim convincing; a careless one may prove detrimental, weakening the writer's credibility.` → 现在分词作结果状语 + 分号连接并列对比句 → 用分词短语补充结果，用分号形成鲜明对照，适合论证型写作。
- `when students discuss social issues, where adverse consequences and significant differences often require careful distinction` → `when` 引导时间状语从句 + `where` 引导非限制性定语从句 → 用 `where` 对抽象情境作补充说明，使句子信息更密集。
- `The effect of precise diction is profound: it enables writers to present constructive proposals rather than empty complaints` → 冒号解释结构 + `enable sb. to do sth.` + `rather than` 对比 → 用冒号引出具体解释，用 `enable...to do` 表达因果，用 `rather than` 形成对比。
- `learners should treat vocabulary expansion as a desirable habit, revise every sentence with care, and avoid the temptation to rely on convenient but imprecise words` → `treat...as...` + 并列谓语 + `avoid the temptation to do` → 用 `treat...as...` 表“视为”，用并列动词提出建议，用 `avoid the temptation to do` 表达否定性劝告。
