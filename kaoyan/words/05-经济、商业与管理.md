## 短文

### 短文
Few issues worry policymakers more than the fear that a mild slowdown may harden into a prolonged recession. When aggregate demand weakens, firms postpone investment and households cut back on consumption, so orders fall and unemployment climbs. Governments usually respond with a fiscal stimulus — higher spending or lower taxes — while central banks cut interest rates in the hope that cheap credit will revive confidence.

Yet such remedies have limits. If public spending is financed by borrowing, the deficit widens and investors may demand higher yields. Subsidies handed to struggling firms can also shield inefficient producers from competition, dampening productivity. Economists therefore disagree about how far the state should intervene. What most of them accept is that sustainable growth depends less on short-term stimulus than on an institutional environment in which entrepreneurs can innovate, workers can retrain, and capital can flow without distortion.

### 中译
很少有比这更让决策者忧虑的事：他们担心一次温和的放缓会恶化为长期衰退。当总需求走弱时，企业推迟投资，家庭削减消费，于是订单下滑、失业上升。政府通常以财政刺激来应对——增加支出或减税——而中央银行则降低利率，希望廉价信贷能重振信心。

然而这类药方有其限度。如果公共支出靠借债来融资，赤字就会扩大，投资者可能索要更高的收益率。给予困境企业的补贴也可能让低效生产者在竞争中受到保护，从而抑制生产率。因此经济学家在国家应干预到何种程度这一点上意见不一。他们多数人认同的是：可持续增长所依赖的，与其说是短期刺激，不如说是一套制度环境——在其中企业家能够创新、工人能够再培训、资本能够无扭曲地流动。

### 语法点
1. 「the fear that a mild slowdown may harden into a prolonged recession」→ 同位语从句 → 抽象名词（fear / fact / belief / evidence / hope）后接 that 从句说明其具体内容，that 在从句中不作成分、一般不可省略；写作时用它把笼统的判断展开成完整命题。
2. 「If public spending is financed by borrowing, the deficit widens」→ 条件状语从句 + 一般现在时被动语态 → 陈述普遍规律或常态时，主句与从句同用一般现在时，不用 will；「is financed by」用被动是为了让公共支出做主语、突出资金来源而不提施动者。
3. 「Subsidies handed to struggling firms can also shield inefficient producers from competition, dampening productivity.」→ 过去分词短语作后置定语 + 现在分词短语作结果状语 → 「handed to...」相当于 which are handed to...，可压缩定语从句；「dampening...」的逻辑主语是前面整个事态，表示自然而然的结果；注意搭配 shield sb./sth. from sth.。
4. 「Economists therefore disagree about how far the state should intervene.」→ 介词 + wh- 引导的宾语从句 → 介词后一般不能直接跟 that 从句（除 in that、except that 等固定形式），但可自由接 how / whether / what 等从句；how far 在此表程度，是考研阅读中常见的名词性从句形式。
5. 「What most of them accept is that sustainable growth depends less on short-term stimulus than on an institutional environment in which entrepreneurs can innovate.」→ 主语从句 + 表语从句 + less...than 比较结构 + 「介词 + which」定语从句 → What 引导主语从句时谓语用单数 is；depend less on A than on B 中 than 后须补出介词 on 以保持平行；in which 相当于 in the environment，用「介词 + which」比 where 更正式，适合书面表达。
