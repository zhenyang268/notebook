## 短文

### 短文
When universities propose to transform their curricula by integrating artificial intelligence, the debate quickly moves beyond technology itself. Supporters advocate such reform because it may promote students’ adaptability and influence how they solve complex problems. Critics, however, oppose the rush to alter traditional courses, arguing that commercial pressure could undermine the educational values that have long shaped academic life. They resist the assumption that every innovation necessarily improves learning, and they warn that constant experimentation may affect teachers’ authority and students’ confidence. A wiser approach is not to reject change altogether but to examine who benefits from it and who bears its costs. Only when institutions support careful trial and open discussion can reform truly serve education rather than merely serve the market.

### 中译
当大学提出通过融入人工智能来改造课程体系时，争论很快便超出了技术本身。支持者倡导这种改革，因为它可能提升学生的适应能力，并影响他们解决复杂问题的方式。然而，批评者反对急于改变传统课程，他们认为商业压力可能侵蚀长期塑造学术生活的教育价值。他们抵制“每一项创新都必然改善学习”的假设，并警告说不断实验可能影响教师的权威和学生的信心。更明智的做法不是完全拒绝变革，而是审视谁从中受益、谁承担其代价。只有当院校支持谨慎试验和公开讨论时，改革才能真正服务于教育，而不只是服务于市场。

### 语法点
- “When universities propose to transform their curricula by integrating artificial intelligence” → When引导时间状语从句；by integrating为“介词+动名词” → 用于交代背景或条件，并用by doing说明方式。
- “arguing that commercial pressure could undermine the educational values that have long shaped academic life” → 现在分词短语作伴随状语；that引导宾语从句，第二个that引导定语从句 → 用分词补充说明批评者的观点，并用嵌套从句增强信息密度。
- “They resist the assumption that every innovation necessarily improves learning” → resist后接名词宾语；that引导同位语从句 → 用that解释assumption的具体内容，resist常表示“抵制某种观念、做法”。
- “A wiser approach is not to reject change altogether but to examine who benefits from it and who bears its costs” → not...but...连接两个不定式；两个who引导并列宾语从句 → 用not...but...构成对比，用who从句分别说明受益者与承担者。
- “Only when institutions support careful trial and open discussion can reform truly serve education rather than merely serve the market” → Only+when状语从句置句首引起主句部分倒装；rather than连接并列动词短语 → 用倒装强调“只有……才……”，用rather than表达取舍与对比。
