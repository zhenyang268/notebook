## 语法短文

### 短文
High-frequency adverbs and prepositional phrases do more than decorate a sentence; they carry its logic. In academic English, writers use words such as however, therefore, and increasingly to signal contrast, cause, and change. Prepositional phrases like in particular, by contrast, and in terms of then specify what the contrast or change applies to. Readers who ignore these markers often mistake a limited claim for a general one. For instance, a sentence that says "in practice" may qualify a rule that looks absolute on the surface. Such phrases are especially common in exam passages, where they guide the reader from evidence to conclusion. If these markers were removed, the text would become harder to follow, even though its grammar might remain correct. What matters, therefore, is not memorizing long lists but noticing how these small units control meaning.

### 译文
1. High-frequency adverbs and prepositional phrases do more than decorate a sentence; they carry its logic. → 高频副词和介词短语不只是给句子做装饰；它们承载句子的逻辑。
2. In academic English, writers use words such as however, therefore, and increasingly to signal contrast, cause, and change. → 在学术英语中，作者使用 however、therefore、increasingly 这类词来标示对比、原因和变化。
3. Prepositional phrases like in particular, by contrast, and in terms of then specify what the contrast or change applies to. → 像 in particular、by contrast、in terms of 这样的介词短语，则进一步明确对比或变化所适用的对象。
4. Readers who ignore these markers often mistake a limited claim for a general one. → 忽视这些标记的读者，常把有限的论断误当成普遍性的论断。
5. For instance, a sentence that says "in practice" may qualify a rule that looks absolute on the surface. → 例如，一个带有 in practice 的句子，可能会限制一条表面上看来绝对的规则。
6. Such phrases are especially common in exam passages, where they guide the reader from evidence to conclusion. → 这类短语在考试文章中尤其常见，它们引导读者从证据走向结论。
7. If these markers were removed, the text would become harder to follow, even though its grammar might remain correct. → 如果去掉这些标记，文本会变得更难理解，即使其语法可能仍然正确。
8. What matters, therefore, is not memorizing long lists but noticing how these small units control meaning. → 因此，重要的不是背长串清单，而是注意这些小单位如何控制意义。

### 语法点
- *Readers who ignore these markers* → 定语从句（who 引导） → 修饰 Readers，限定“忽视这些标记的读者”，使判断对象明确。
- *to signal contrast, cause, and change* → 不定式短语（非谓语） → 作目的状语，说明作者使用这些副词的目的。
- *not memorizing long lists but noticing how these small units control meaning* → not...but... 对比强调结构 → 否定“背长清单”，突出“注意小单位如何控制意义”这一重点。
- *If these markers were removed, the text would become harder to follow* → 虚拟条件句（含被动 were removed） → 假设标记被去掉，说明其对文本可读性的作用。
- *from evidence to conclusion* → 介词短语（方向状语） → 说明读者被引导的路径，使“从证据到结论”的关系具体化。
