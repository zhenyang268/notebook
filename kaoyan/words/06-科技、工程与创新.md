## 语法短文

Engineering innovation is often measured by novelty, but novelty alone rarely produces useful technology. A device may work in a laboratory and still fail in everyday use, where maintenance, energy supply and user behavior matter. For this reason, engineers increasingly treat testing as part of design rather than as a final step. If a system is not reliable, its advanced features may become a burden. Consider public transport software: a small delay in data processing can spread through the network and affect thousands of passengers. Designers therefore build redundancies and monitor performance after launch. What makes innovation valuable is not a single breakthrough but a long process of improvement. The most valuable projects are those that combine technical ambition with careful evaluation and clear responsibility.

**译文**

1. 工程创新常常以新颖性来衡量，但仅凭新颖性很少能产出有用的技术。
2. 一个装置可能在实验室里运转正常，却仍在日常使用中失败；在日常使用中，维护、能源供应和用户行为都很重要。
3. 因此，工程师越来越多地把测试视为设计的一部分，而不是最后一步。
4. 如果一个系统不可靠，它的先进功能就可能成为负担。
5. 以公共交通软件为例：数据处理中的微小延迟可能在整个网络中扩散，并影响数千名乘客。
6. 因此，设计者会构建冗余，并在上线后监测性能。
7. 使创新有价值的不是单次突破，而是长期的改进过程。
8. 最有价值的项目，是那些把技术雄心与审慎评估、明确责任结合起来的项目。

**语法点**

- *Engineering innovation is often measured by novelty* → 被动语态（一般现在时） → 说明“创新”通常以新颖性为衡量标准，并引出下文对单一新颖性的质疑。
- *where maintenance, energy supply and user behavior matter* → 关系副词 where 引导的定语从句 → 修饰 everyday use，说明真实使用场景中维护、供能和用户行为等条件。
- *treat testing as part of design* → 动名词短语作宾语 → 将 testing 名词化，表达工程实践把测试纳入设计流程。
- *If a system is not reliable* → 条件状语从句 → 设定可靠性前提，说明先进功能可能反成负担。
- *What makes innovation valuable is not a single breakthrough but a long process of improvement* → 主语从句（wh- cleft 强调结构） → 强调创新价值来自持续改进，而非单次突破。
