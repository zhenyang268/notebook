## 短文

### 短文
The past decade has witnessed how **digital** technology reshapes **engineering** practice. What once required months of manual calculation can now be finished within hours, thanks to **algorithms** that **simulate** entire production lines before a single component is made. Engineers no longer treat a **prototype** as a costly luxury; they build, test and discard dozens, each failure feeding data back into the design.

Yet **innovation** is not merely a matter of faster machines. A **breakthrough** in the laboratory remains worthless unless it can be scaled up, and scaling up depends on **infrastructure**, capital and, above all, people willing to abandon methods that have grown **obsolete**. Firms that merely **upgrade** existing equipment often lose ground to rivals that rethink the process from scratch. The real competitive edge lies less in software than in the habits of mind that decide how fast a firm can learn and unlearn.

### 中译
过去十年见证了数字技术如何重塑工程实践。曾经需要数月人工计算的工作，如今几小时内即可完成，这得益于能在单个零件制造之前就模拟整条生产线的算法。工程师不再把原型视为昂贵的奢侈品；他们制造、测试并丢弃几十个原型，每一次失败都为设计回馈数据。

然而，创新不仅仅是机器更快的问题。实验室里的突破若不能扩大规模便毫无价值，而扩大规模取决于基础设施、资本，尤其是取决于那些愿意抛弃已变得过时的方法的人。仅仅升级现有设备的公司，往往会在那些从头重新思考流程的对手面前落败。真正的竞争优势较少在于软件本身，更多在于决定一家企业能多快学习与遗忘的思维习惯。

### 语法点
1. `The past decade has witnessed how digital technology reshapes engineering practice.` → 现在完成时 + 时间名词作主语（拟人化）+ how 引导宾语从句 → 用于开篇引出某一趋势或变化，作文首句可直接套用「The past decade has witnessed how...」。

2. `What once required months of manual calculation can now be finished within hours` → what 引导主语从句作整句主语 + 被动语态 → 主语从句使句子更有分量，常与 once/now 对比，用于今昔对照。

3. `thanks to algorithms that simulate entire production lines` → thanks to + 名词短语 + that 引导定语从句 → thanks to 表原因（后可接名词或名词短语），定语从句限定前面的名词，避免另起一句。

4. `they build, test and discard dozens, each failure feeding data back into the design` → 分号/逗号连接的并列句 + 独立主格结构（each failure feeding...）→ 独立主格作伴随状语，补充说明主句结果，是考研翻译与写作中的高分结构。

5. `A breakthrough ... remains worthless unless it can be scaled up` → unless 引导否定条件状语从句 + 被动语态 → unless 相当于 if...not，用于强调「唯有……才……」的必要条件，写作中可替代 if...not 提升句式。
