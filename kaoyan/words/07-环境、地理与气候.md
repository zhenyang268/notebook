## 语法短文

Few places on Earth show the cost of warming as plainly as the high plateaus of Central Asia. There, glaciers that once fed a dozen rivers are shrinking year by year. If the ice disappears, hundreds of millions of people downstream will lose the water on which their farms depend.

What makes the problem harder to solve is that no single country controls the outcome. The rivers cross borders, and so do the dust clouds that rise from dried lake beds. Only when neighbouring states share data and monitor the same basins can any of them adapt in time.

Scientists have warned for decades that the region is drying. Their warnings were ignored, partly because the changes came slowly. Yet slow change, once it passes a threshold, becomes sudden collapse — and by then the options left are few.

**译文**

1. Few places on Earth show the cost of warming as plainly as the high plateaus of Central Asia.
→ 地球上很少有地方能像中亚高原那样，把气候变暖的代价展现得如此直白。

2. There, glaciers that once fed a dozen rivers are shrinking year by year.
→ 在那里，曾经补给十几条河流的冰川正一年年萎缩。

3. If the ice disappears, hundreds of millions of people downstream will lose the water on which their farms depend.
→ 一旦这些冰消失，下游数亿人将失去他们的农田所依赖的水源。

4. What makes the problem harder to solve is that no single country controls the outcome.
→ 使问题更难解决的是，没有任何一个国家能单独掌控结果。

5. The rivers cross borders, and so do the dust clouds that rise from dried lake beds.
→ 河流跨越国界，从干涸湖床上升起的沙尘云也是如此。

6. Only when neighbouring states share data and monitor the same basins can any of them adapt in time.
→ 只有邻国共享数据、共同监测同一片流域，其中任何一国才能及时作出适应。

7. Scientists have warned for decades that the region is drying.
→ 几十年来，科学家一直警告说该地区正在变干。

8. Their warnings were ignored, partly because the changes came slowly.
→ 他们的警告被忽视了，部分原因是变化来得缓慢。

9. Yet slow change, once it passes a threshold, becomes sudden collapse — and by then the options left are few.
→ 然而缓慢的变化一旦越过某个临界点，就会变成突然的崩溃——到那时，剩下的选择就不多了。

**语法点**

1. *that once fed a dozen rivers* → 定语从句（关系代词 that 引导，修饰 glaciers）→ 限定说明是"哪一类冰川"，用一次"曾经"与现在的"萎缩"形成时间对照，为主句提供背景。

2. *What makes the problem harder to solve* → 主语从句（what 引导的名词性从句作整句主语）→ 把"使问题棘手的原因"打包成一个整体先提出，后面 is that... 才能顺势给出答案，避免头重脚轻的并列堆砌。

3. *the options left* → 非谓语：过去分词短语作后置定语 → 相当于 the options that are left，省去定语从句，让句尾收得短促，与"崩溃"的紧迫感相配。

4. *Only when neighbouring states share data and monitor the same basins can any of them adapt in time* → 部分倒装（only + 状语从句置于句首，主句助动词 can 提前）→ 强调"共享数据"是唯一前提，语气比正常语序强得多；同时把 adapt in time 留在句末作为信息焦点。

5. *Their warnings were ignored* → 被动语态（一般过去时）→ 隐去施动者，让"警告被忽视"这一事实本身成为陈述重心；若改成主动句，就必须交代"谁忽视"，反而分散论点。
