## 短文

### 短文

Few issues illustrate the cost of short-sighted development better than the degradation of the planet's ecosystems. As forests are cleared for farmland, the habitats they once supported vanish, and with them the biodiversity that keeps soils fertile and water cycles stable. Burning fossil fuels adds a further burden: carbon emissions trap heat, rainfall patterns shift, and regions already prone to drought face longer dry seasons, while steep slopes stripped of vegetation lose their topsoil to erosion.

Yet the picture is not entirely bleak. Renewable energy now costs less than coal in many markets, and conservation projects — from wetland restoration to urban tree planting — have shown that damaged land can recover. What matters is whether governments treat sustainability as a long-term investment rather than an expense to be postponed.

### 中译

很少有议题能比地球生态系统的退化更能说明短视发展的代价。随着森林被砍伐变为农田，它们曾经供养的栖息地随之消失，维持土壤肥沃与水文循环稳定的生物多样性也一并消失。燃烧化石燃料又加重了负担：碳排放使热量积聚，降雨格局发生改变，本就易受干旱的地区旱季变得更长，而植被被剥光的陡坡则因侵蚀而流失表土。

然而，情况并非全然黯淡。在许多市场上，可再生能源的成本如今已低于煤炭；而从湿地修复到城市植树，各类保护项目已经证明，受损的土地能够恢复。真正重要的是，各国政府是否把可持续性视为长期投资，而不是一项可以推迟的开支。

### 语法点

1. `Few issues illustrate the cost ... better than the degradation of ... ecosystems.` → 否定词 Few + 比较级 better than，用比较结构表达最高级含义 → 写作中想强调"最……"时，可用 Few/Nothing + 比较级 + than 的句式，比直接用 the most 更有力度。

2. `As forests are cleared for farmland, the habitats they once supported vanish` → as 引导时间/伴随状语从句（被动）+ 省略关系代词的限制性定语从句 they once supported → 定语从句先行词为 habitats，关系词作 supported 的宾语故可省略；注意主句谓语 vanish 与从句时态、单复数的对应。

3. `and with them the biodiversity that keeps soils fertile and water cycles stable` → 介词短语 with them 置于句首引起的倒装（谓语 vanish 承前省略）+ that 引导定语从句 + keep + 宾语 + 形容词宾语补足语 → and with them... 是英语中常见的"随之而来"倒装追加结构，考研阅读长句中常作补充信息。

4. `regions already prone to drought face longer dry seasons, while steep slopes stripped of vegetation lose their topsoil to erosion` → while 引导对比状语从句（表"而、然而"）+ 形容词短语 prone to drought 后置定语 + 过去分词短语 stripped of vegetation 后置定语 → 后置定语是考研长句加长的主要手段之一，翻译时前置为"……的"。

5. `What matters is whether governments treat sustainability as a long-term investment rather than an expense to be postponed.` → 主语从句 What matters + 表语从句 whether... + treat sth. as sth. + rather than 并列 + 不定式被动 to be postponed 作后置定语 → 议论文结尾下结论的经典句式，可用于写作模板：What matters is whether...
