## 短文

### 短文
Universities are under growing pressure to demonstrate that their curricula equip undergraduates with more than job skills. In many departments, assessment now combines written examinations with seminars, group projects and continuous coursework, so that a single score no longer determines a student’s future. Yet the credibility of this system depends on academic integrity. When plagiarism is treated merely as a minor offence, the value of a degree is eroded and honest scholarship suffers. Accordingly, supervisors should train students to acknowledge sources accurately, while academic work should rely on peer review rather than reputation. A thesis or dissertation, in particular, tests whether candidates can frame a question, evaluate evidence and defend an argument before experts. If universities take these practices seriously, they will produce graduates capable of independent thought—not merely skilled test-takers.

### 中译
大学正承受着越来越大的压力，须证明其课程赋予本科生的不只是职业技能。在许多院系，考核如今把笔试与研讨会、小组项目和持续性课程作业结合起来，因此单一分数不再决定学生的未来。然而，这一制度的可信度取决于学术诚信。当抄袭仅被当作轻微违规时，学位的价值便受到侵蚀，诚实的学术研究也会受损。因此，导师应训练学生准确注明来源，而学术工作应依靠同行评审而非名气。尤其是学位论文，它检验候选人能否提出问题、评估证据并在专家面前为自己的论点辩护。如果大学认真对待这些做法，它们将培养出能够独立思考的毕业生——而不仅仅是熟练的应试者。

### 语法点
1. `Universities are under growing pressure to demonstrate that their curricula equip undergraduates with more than job skills.` → `be under pressure to do` + `that` 宾语从句；`curricula` 为复数主语。 → 表达“承受做某事的压力”，`that` 从句用陈述语序，注意从句主谓一致。
2. `assessment now combines written examinations with seminars, group projects and continuous coursework, so that a single score no longer determines a student’s future.` → `combine A with B`；`so that` 引导结果状语从句。 → `combine...with...` 表示“把A与B结合”；`so that` 引出结果，常与 `no longer`、情态动词等搭配。
3. `When plagiarism is treated merely as a minor offence, the value of a degree is eroded and honest scholarship suffers.` → `When` 引导时间/条件状语从句 + 被动语态 `be treated as`；主句并列谓语。 → `treat A as B` 意为“把A视为B”，被动式保留 `as`；`when` 可译为“当……时/如果……”。
4. `supervisors should train students to acknowledge sources accurately, while academic work should rely on peer review rather than reputation.` → `train sb to do`；`while` 表对比；`rely on`；`rather than` 连接平行成分。 → `train sb to do` 表示“训练某人做”；`while` 连接分句表对比；`rather than` 后接名词/动名词，与前面成分保持平行。
5. `A thesis or dissertation, in particular, tests whether candidates can frame a question, evaluate evidence and defend an argument before experts.` → 插入语 `in particular`；`whether` 宾语从句；情态动词 `can` 后三个动词并列。 → `in particular` 用于强调；`whether` 从句作 `tests` 的宾语；并列动词共享 `can`，形式保持一致。
