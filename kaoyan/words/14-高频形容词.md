## 短文

### 短文
When students assess an argument in a reading passage, it is essential to distinguish between a significant claim and a merely impressive one. A reliable judgment depends on adequate evidence, yet evidence is often complex and incomplete. It is therefore appropriate to ask whether the writer has offered considerable support or has simply repeated an obvious point. An effective reader does not treat every detail as equally important; instead, he or she weighs the precise function of each sentence. This habit is not an inevitable gift, but it can be learned. If a conclusion seems too strong, a careful reader will notice the gap between the evidence and the claim. Such caution is essential in examinations, where a significant mistake may result from a hasty judgment rather than from a lack of knowledge.

### 中译
当学生在阅读文章中评估一个论证时，关键是要区分一个重要主张与一个仅仅给人深刻印象的主张。可靠的判断取决于充分的证据，而证据往往复杂且不完整。因此，恰当的做是追问作者是否提供了相当多的支持，还是只是重复了一个显而易见的观点。高效的读者不会把每个细节都视为同等重要；相反，他/她会权衡每个句子的确切作用。这种习惯并非一种不可避免的天赋，而是可以习得的。如果一个结论显得过于强硬，细心的读者会注意到证据与主张之间的差距。这种谨慎在考试中至关重要，因为在考试中，一个重大错误可能源于草率判断，而非知识欠缺。

### 语法点
- `it is essential to distinguish between a significant claim and a merely impressive one` → it作形式主语，不定式短语作真正主语；distinguish between A and B → 评价类形容词常用“It is + adj. + to do”结构；between...and...连接并列宾语，表示区分两者。
- `whether the writer has offered considerable support or has simply repeated an obvious point` → whether...or...引导宾语从句，or连接两个并列谓语has offered与has repeated → 用于表达两种可能或选择；并列谓语保持时态一致。
- `does not treat every detail as equally important` → 助动词does not + 动词原形；treat...as...复合结构 → 否定一般现在时；treat A as B表示“把A当作B”。
- `This habit is not an inevitable gift, but it can be learned.` → but连接两个并列分句；can be learned为情态动词被动语态 → 用but表转折对比；被动语态强调习惯可以被习得。
- `where a significant mistake may result from a hasty judgment rather than from a lack of knowledge` → where引导非限制性定语从句；result from表示原因；rather than连接两个from短语 → 非限制性定语从句补充说明情境；rather than连接平行成分，表达取舍对比。
