## 语法短文

## 短文

A legal system earns public trust not by proclaiming rights but by making them enforceable. Rights that exist only on paper offer little protection to ordinary citizens. To turn principles into practice, a state must establish transparent procedures, independent courts, and accessible remedies. When officials act beyond their legal authority, citizens should be able to challenge those decisions before an impartial tribunal. It is this accountability, rather than the mere wording of statutes, that gives law its moral force. If judicial review were weak or easily ignored, constitutional promises would soon become empty slogans. Rights become real only when they can be claimed, tested, and enforced. Therefore, a credible institution protects individual liberty by limiting power and by subjecting every exercise of authority to public scrutiny. Such scrutiny does not weaken government; it makes government more legitimate.

## 译文

1. 一个法律体系赢得公众信任，不是靠宣扬权利，而是靠使权利可执行。
2. 只停留在纸面上的权利，对普通公民提供的保护很少。
3. 为了把原则转化为实践，国家必须建立透明的程序、独立的法院和可获得的救济。
4. 当官员超越其法定权限行事时，公民应当能够在公正的法庭上质疑这些决定。
5. 正是这种问责性，而不是法规的单纯措辞，赋予法律道德力量。
6. 如果司法审查薄弱或容易被忽视，宪法承诺很快就会变成空洞口号。
7. 权利只有在能够被主张、检验和执行时才会成为现实。
8. 因此，一个可信的制度通过限制权力、使每一次权力行使都接受公众监督来保护个人自由。
9. 这种监督不会削弱政府；它使政府更具正当性。

## 语法点

1. *Rights that exist only on paper* → 限制性定语从句（that 引导，修饰 Rights） → 说明“仅存在于纸面”的权利，为后文“保护有限”提供限定条件。
2. *To turn principles into practice* → 动词不定式短语作目的状语（非谓语） → 点明国家建立透明程序、独立法院与可及救济的目的。
3. *It is this accountability ... that gives law its moral force.* → 强调句（It is ... that ...） → 把“问责性”而非“法规措辞”置于信息焦点，突出法律道德力量的真正来源。
4. *If judicial review were weak or easily ignored, constitutional promises would soon become empty slogans.* → 虚拟条件句（与现在事实相反） → 假设司法审查薄弱或易被忽视，推出宪法承诺会落空的后果。
5. *they can be claimed, tested, and enforced* → 被动语态（情态动词 can + be + 过去分词） → 说明权利不是抽象宣示，而须能够被主张、检验和执行。
