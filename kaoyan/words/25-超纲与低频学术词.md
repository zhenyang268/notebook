## 短文

### 短文
Recent scholarship on academic innovation has challenged the prevailing **paradigm** that **empirical** progress depends mainly on individual brilliance. A more plausible **heuristic** treats discovery as a social process: **salient** anomalies are noticed, discussed, and then **corroborated** by independent researchers. **Intrinsic** motivation matters, but **exogenous** incentives—funding, prestige, and institutional pressure—can either accelerate or distort this process. The **proliferation** of preprint platforms, albeit useful, has made the distinction between preliminary findings and settled knowledge less clear. A useful **caveat** is therefore that the **dichotomy** between “pure” and “applied” research is often overstated. Hitherto marginal voices may also reshape what counts as evidence. Researchers should thus remain cautious about claiming that any single method can capture the complex ecology of knowledge production.

### 中译
近期关于学术创新的研究挑战了一种流行范式，即实证进展主要依赖个人才华。一个更合理的启发式方法把发现视为社会过程：显著异常被注意、讨论，随后由独立研究者证实。内在动机固然重要，但外部激励——经费、声望和制度压力——可能加速也可能扭曲这一过程。预印本平台的激增虽然有用，却使初步发现与确定知识之间的界限变得更不清晰。因此一个有用的提醒是：“纯”研究与“应用”研究之间的二分法常被夸大。此前边缘化的声音也可能重塑何为证据。因此，研究者应保持谨慎，不要声称任何单一方法都能捕捉知识生产的复杂生态。

### 语法点
- `has challenged the prevailing paradigm that empirical progress depends mainly on individual brilliance` → 现在完成时 + that 引导同位语从句（说明 paradigm 的内容） → 写作中可用“claim/idea/paradigm + that...”把抽象名词具体化。
- `treats discovery as a social process` → treat + 宾语 + as + 名词（宾语补足语） → 用于给某事物重新定性，如 regard/view/describe...as...
- `are noticed, discussed, and then corroborated by independent researchers` → 三个过去分词并列的被动结构 → 描述研究流程时用并列被动，保持主语一致、动作递进。
- `albeit useful` → albeit + 形容词/名词短语，表让步 → 比 although 更书面，常插在句中作补充，如 The method, albeit costly, remains...
- `has made the distinction between preliminary findings and settled knowledge less clear` → make + 宾语 + 形容词补足语（复合宾语），其中宾语含 between...and... 介词短语 → 用于表达“使某判断/界限变得……”，适合学术评价句。
