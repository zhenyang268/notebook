## 短文

### 短文
Cultural **heritage** is never a mere collection of old objects; it also includes **intangible** traditions that communities **preserve** and **transmit** across generations. In an age when **digital media** can **disseminate** a local practice to a global **audience** within hours, creators and institutions face a delicate question: how can a tradition maintain its **authenticity** while reaching people who have never encountered it? Some argue that the **aesthetic** value of a work should not be sacrificed to popular taste. Others believe that a **medium** is not a neutral container but shapes what it carries, so an object designed for intimate use may lose meaning when it is adapted for mass entertainment. The most persuasive response is perhaps neither to freeze heritage in a museum nor to surrender it to the market, but to let communities themselves decide which elements are essential and which may change.

### 中译
文化遗产从来不只是旧物的集合；它还包括社区代代保存和传承的非物质传统。在一个数字媒体能在数小时内把一种地方实践传播给全球受众的时代，创作者和机构面临一个微妙的问题：一种传统在触及从未接触过它的人时，如何保持其本真性？一些人认为，一件作品的审美价值不应被牺牲给大众趣味。另一些人认为，媒介不是中性的容器，而会塑造它所承载的内容，因此一件为亲密使用而设计的物品在被改编为大众娱乐时可能失去意义。最有说服力的回应或许既不是把遗产冻结在博物馆里，也不是把它交给市场，而是让社区自己决定哪些元素是根本性的、哪些可以改变。

### 语法点
- “intangible traditions that communities preserve and transmit across generations” → that 引导定语从句，先行词为 traditions，that 在从句中作 preserve and transmit 的宾语 → 用于给抽象名词补充“谁保存、谁传承”的具体信息。
- “In an age when digital media can disseminate a local practice to a global audience within hours” → 介词短语 In an age + when 引导定语从句修饰 age → 用于开篇设定时代背景，when 从句中主谓宾结构完整。
- “how can a tradition maintain its authenticity while reaching people who have never encountered it?” → how 引导特殊疑问句；while + 现在分词作时间/伴随状语；who 引导定语从句修饰 people → 用于提出核心问题，while doing 的逻辑主语与主句主语一致。
- “a medium is not a neutral container but shapes what it carries” → not...but... 并列对比结构；what it carries 为宾语从句作 shapes 的宾语 → 用于先否定常见看法、再提出真正观点；what 从句相当于“它所承载的东西”。
- “neither to freeze heritage in a museum nor to surrender it to the market, but to let communities themselves decide which elements are essential” → neither...nor...but... 并列不定式结构；let sb. do sth. 中 decide 前省略 to；which 引导宾语从句作 decide 的宾语 → 用于三项并列取舍，突出最终主张；themselves 作同位语加强语气。
