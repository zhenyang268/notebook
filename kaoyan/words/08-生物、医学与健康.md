## 语法短文

Few public-health problems are as quietly dangerous as antibiotic resistance. When a patient takes an antibiotic for a common cold, the drug does nothing to the virus; instead, it kills harmless bacteria and gives resistant strains room to multiply. Were such misuse rare, the consequences would be modest. In reality, roughly half of all prescriptions in some countries are unnecessary, and in agriculture the same drugs are fed to healthy animals to speed growth. What makes the situation urgent is that no new class of antibiotics has reached the clinic for decades. Unless doctors and farmers change their habits, minor infections could again become lethal. Patients, too, must accept that a prescription is not a proof of care. The solution is not to abandon the drugs but to use them sparingly — a principle that medicine has learned slowly and forgotten quickly.

**译文**

1. Few public-health problems are as quietly dangerous as antibiotic resistance.
很少有公共卫生问题像抗生素耐药性那样，危险得如此悄无声息。

2. When a patient takes an antibiotic for a common cold, the drug does nothing to the virus; instead, it kills harmless bacteria and gives resistant strains room to multiply.
当病人因普通感冒服用抗生素时，这种药对病毒毫无作用；它杀死的是无害细菌，反而给耐药菌株腾出了繁殖的空间。

3. Were such misuse rare, the consequences would be modest.
假如这种误用很罕见，后果本不会严重。

4. In reality, roughly half of all prescriptions in some countries are unnecessary, and in agriculture the same drugs are fed to healthy animals to speed growth.
而在现实中，某些国家约有一半的处方是不必要的；在养殖业，同样的药物被喂给健康的动物，用来加快生长。

5. What makes the situation urgent is that no new class of antibiotics has reached the clinic for decades.
使局面变得紧迫的是：几十年来，没有一类新的抗生素进入临床。

6. Unless doctors and farmers change their habits, minor infections could again become lethal.
除非医生和养殖者改变习惯，否则轻微的感染也可能再次致命。

7. Patients, too, must accept that a prescription is not a proof of care.
患者也必须接受一点：一张处方并不等于一份关怀。

8. The solution is not to abandon the drugs but to use them sparingly — a principle that medicine has learned slowly and forgotten quickly.
解决办法不是弃用这些药物，而是谨慎地使用它们——这条原则，医学学得很慢，却忘得很快。

**语法点**

1. *What makes the situation urgent* → what 引导的主语从句（名词性从句）→ 整个从句在主句中充当主语，把"使局面紧迫的那个因素"打包成一个名词性单位，再由 is 后面的表语从句给出答案，形成"提问—揭晓"的信息推进。

2. *When a patient takes an antibiotic for a common cold* → when 引导的时间状语从句 → 为后面由分号连接的两个分句同时设定时间与情境背景，避免重复交代条件，使句子结构紧凑。

3. *Were such misuse rare* → 省略 if 的虚拟条件倒装（were 提前）→ 表达与现在事实相反的假设，等于 If such misuse were rare；倒装使语气更正式、更书面，同时把"罕见"这一前提提前到句首加以凸显。

4. *not to abandon the drugs but to use them sparingly* → 不定式短语作表语（非谓语结构）→ 说明主语 the solution 的具体内容；not... but... 构成对照，把"弃用"与"慎用"两种做法明确区分开。

5. *are fed to healthy animals* → 被动语态 → 把受事者 healthy animals 放在句末的焦点位置，而施动者（养殖者）因不言自明而省略，使陈述显得客观、像在描述制度性做法而非指责某个人。
