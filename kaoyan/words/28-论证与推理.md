## 短文

### 短文
When a scholar argues that a policy will succeed, the argument rarely rests on a single fact. It usually begins with a premise that most readers already accept, gathers evidence from several sources, and then draws an inference that goes beyond what the data alone can show. This step is where reasoning most often fails. A hypothesis may look plausible, yet a closer examination can refute it: one counterexample is enough to contradict a claim that was presented as universal. Careful writers therefore demonstrate rather than assert, and they justify each link in the chain instead of asking readers to take it on trust. Coherence matters as much as truth, for a conclusion that does not follow from its premises cannot be called valid, however confidently it is stated.

### 中译
当一位学者论证某项政策会成功时，这一论证很少只依赖单一事实。它通常从一个大多数读者已然接受的前提开始，从若干来源收集证据，然后推出一个超出数据本身所能显示的推论。正是在这一步，推理最常出问题。一个假说也许看上去合情合理，但更细致的考察就能驳倒它：一个反例就足以推翻一个被当作普遍命题提出的主张。因此，严谨的作者会去证明，而非仅仅断言；他们会为链条上的每一环给出理由，而不是要求读者不加质疑地相信。连贯性与真实性同样重要，因为一个并非从其前提推出的结论，无论表述得多么自信，都不能被称为有效。

### 语法点
1. `the argument rarely rests on a single fact` → 主谓 + 介词短语，中位否定副词 rarely 置于实义动词前 → 写论证句时用 rarely/seldom/hardly 弱化绝对化表述，比 not 更学术、更留有余地。
2. `draws an inference that goes beyond what the data alone can show` → that 引导定语从句，从句内嵌套 what 引导的介词宾语从句（beyond 的宾语）→ 考场长句常用「动词 + 名词 + that…what…」双层嵌套，翻译时把 what 从句译成「……所……的」。
3. `This step is where reasoning most often fails` → where 引导表语从句，先行词为表抽象地点的 step → 用于「X is where…」句式指出问题所在，比 "X is the point at which" 更简洁。
4. `one counterexample is enough to contradict a claim that was presented as universal` → be + adj. + enough to do 不定式结构 + that 引导被动定语从句 → enough to do 表「足以」，写作中替代 can 显得更有力；被动定语从句把施动者隐去，适合客观陈述。
5. `Coherence matters as much as truth, for a conclusion that does not follow from its premises cannot be called valid, however confidently it is stated` → for 作并列连词引出原因分句 + however + 副词引导让步状语从句，从句用「however + adv. + 主谓」的正常语序（非倒装）→ 议论文中 for 比 because 更书面；however confidently it is stated 提炼为「无论多么……」，可套用于任何让步收尾句。
