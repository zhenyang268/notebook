## 语法短文

**短文**

A common weakness in examination essays is the overuse of general verbs such as “make,” “get,” and “have,” which blur the writer’s argument and weaken its persuasiveness. To produce a clear claim, candidates should choose precise verbs that reveal the logical relation between ideas: “demonstrate” introduces evidence, “undermine” signals a challenge, and “facilitate” explains a causal role. It is not enough merely to replace simple words with long ones; what matters is whether the verb accurately matches the action being described. If writers were to test each verb against the question “Who does what to whom?”, many vague sentences would immediately become sharper. Only when this habit is practised under timed conditions can it become reliable in the actual examination. Thus, mastering a small set of core verbs is far more useful than memorising long lists without context.

**译文**

A common weakness in examination essays is the overuse of general verbs such as “make,” “get,” and “have,” which blur the writer’s argument and weaken its persuasiveness. → 考试作文中一个常见弱点是滥用“make”“get”“have”等泛化动词，它们会模糊作者的论点并削弱说服力。

To produce a clear claim, candidates should choose precise verbs that reveal the logical relation between ideas: “demonstrate” introduces evidence, “undermine” signals a challenge, and “facilitate” explains a causal role. → 为了提出清晰的主张，考生应选择能揭示观点间逻辑关系的精确动词：“demonstrate”引出证据，“undermine”标示挑战，“facilitate”解释因果作用。

It is not enough merely to replace simple words with long ones; what matters is whether the verb accurately matches the action being described. → 仅仅用长词替换简单词是不够的；真正重要的是动词是否准确匹配所描述的动作。

If writers were to test each verb against the question “Who does what to whom?”, many vague sentences would immediately become sharper. → 如果写作者用“谁对谁做了什么？”这一问题检验每个动词，许多模糊句子会立刻变得更清晰。

Only when this habit is practised under timed conditions can it become reliable in the actual examination. → 只有在限时条件下练习这一习惯，它才能在真实考试中变得可靠。

Thus, mastering a small set of core verbs is far more useful than memorising long lists without context. → 因此，掌握一小组核心动词远比脱离语境背诵长列表更有用。

**语法点**

1. *which blur the writer’s argument and weaken its persuasiveness* → 非限制性定语从句 → 修饰前面的 general verbs，补充说明泛化动词会模糊论点、削弱说服力的后果。
2. *To produce a clear claim* → 不定式短语作目的状语 → 置于句首说明考生选择精确动词的目的，使论证目标更明确。
3. *what matters is whether the verb accurately matches the action being described* → 主语从句 + 表语从句 → `what matters`作主语，`whether...`作表语，构成“真正重要的是……”的判断框架；`being described`为被动非谓语，修饰 action。
4. *Only when this habit is practised under timed conditions can it become reliable in the actual examination* → only + 状语从句置句首引起的部分倒装（含被动） → 强调限时练习这一条件，主句助动词 can 提前；被动 `is practised`突出习惯需要被反复训练。
5. *If writers were to test each verb against the question..., many vague sentences would immediately become sharper* → 虚拟条件句（were to + would do） → 假设写作者未来检验每个动词的情形，说明这种检验会带来的效果，语气客观而非陈述事实。
