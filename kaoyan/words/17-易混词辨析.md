## 短文

### 短文
When a university adopts a new assessment policy, its principal aim should be to ensure fairness rather than merely to raise scores. If teachers adapt the rules carelessly, the change may affect students' confidence and produce an effect contrary to expectation. The guiding principle is simple: any reform must complement, not replace, careful teaching. A sincere compliment from a tutor can also reassure learners, but it cannot assure them of success unless the system itself is sound. Administrators should therefore explain the plan clearly and assure students that their concerns will be heard. Only when policy and practice are adapted to local conditions can the intended effect be achieved, and only then will students trust the principles behind the reform.

### 中译
当一所大学采用新的评估政策时，其主要目的应是确保公平，而不只是提高分数。如果教师草率地调整规则，这一变化可能影响学生的信心，并产生与预期相反的效果。指导原则很简单：任何改革都必须补充而非取代细致的教学。导师真诚的赞美也能让学习者安心，但除非制度本身健全，它不能向他们保证成功。因此，管理者应清楚地解释计划，并向学生保证他们的关切会被倾听。只有当政策与实践适应本地条件时，预期效果才能实现；也只有到那时，学生才会信任改革背后的原则。

### 语法点
1. `its principal aim should be to ensure fairness rather than merely to raise scores` → 系表结构 + 不定式作表语；`rather than` 连接两个平行不定式 → 用于说明“应当做甲而不是乙”，注意前后形式保持一致。
2. `If teachers adapt the rules carelessly, the change may affect students' confidence and produce an effect contrary to expectation` → `if` 引导条件状语从句 + 主句含情态动词 `may` 和并列谓语 → 条件句用一般现在时表条件，主句用 `may` 表可能结果；`contrary to expectation` 作后置定语修饰 `effect`。
3. `any reform must complement, not replace, careful teaching` → `must` + 并列动词 + 插入否定结构 `not replace` → 表示“必须补充而不是取代”，否定部分与前面的动词保持并列。
4. `A sincere compliment from a tutor can also reassure learners, but it cannot assure them of success unless the system itself is sound` → `but` 连接并列分句 + `unless` 引导条件状语从句；`assure sb. of sth.` 结构 → `unless` 相当于“除非”，`assure sb. of sth.` 表示“向某人保证某事”。
5. `Only when policy and practice are adapted to local conditions can the intended effect be achieved` → `Only when...` 置于句首引起主句部分倒装 + 被动语态 → 强调“只有……才……”，主句把情态动词/助动词提前；`be adapted to` 表示“适应”。
