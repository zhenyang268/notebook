## 语法短文

Many learners confuse words such as affect and effect, or adapt and adopt, because they look similar and often appear in the same contexts. A useful solution is to study such words in pairs. When two confusable items are placed side by side, their differences become easier to notice, and learners are less likely to store them as one vague memory. Pairing alone, however, does not guarantee accuracy. If a pair is memorized without examples, the distinction may disappear soon after the lesson. What makes paired learning effective is comparison followed by use: learners should explain how the two words differ, write one sentence for each, and then recall both from meaning rather than from form. Only in this way can similar words be kept apart in long-term memory.

**译文**

1. Many learners confuse words such as affect and effect, or adapt and adopt, because they look similar and often appear in the same contexts. → 许多学习者混淆 affect 和 effect、adapt 和 adopt 这类词，因为它们看起来相似，且经常出现在相同语境中。
2. A useful solution is to study such words in pairs. → 一个有用的解决办法是把这类词成对学习。
3. When two confusable items are placed side by side, their differences become easier to notice, and learners are less likely to store them as one vague memory. → 当两个易混项目并排放置时，它们的差异更容易被注意到，学习者也更不可能把它们储存成一个模糊记忆。
4. Pairing alone, however, does not guarantee accuracy. → 然而，仅靠配对并不能保证准确性。
5. If a pair is memorized without examples, the distinction may disappear soon after the lesson. → 如果一对词在没有例句的情况下被记忆，这种区分可能在课后不久就消失。
6. What makes paired learning effective is comparison followed by use: learners should explain how the two words differ, write one sentence for each, and then recall both from meaning rather than from form. → 使成对学习有效的是比较之后加以使用：学习者应解释这两个词有何不同，各写一个句子，然后根据意义而不是形式回忆两者。
7. Only in this way can similar words be kept apart in long-term memory. → 只有这样，相似的词才能在长期记忆中被区分开。

**语法点**

- *because they look similar and often appear in the same contexts* → 原因状语从句 → 解释学习者为何容易混淆这些易混词。
- *to study such words in pairs* → 动词不定式短语作表语 → 说明“有用的解决办法”具体是什么。
- *When two confusable items are placed side by side* → 时间状语从句（含被动语态） → 交代成对学习发生的时间或条件。
- *comparison followed by use* → 过去分词短语作后置定语 → 修饰 comparison，说明比较之后还要接着使用。
- *Only in this way can similar words be kept apart in long-term memory* → only + 介词短语置于句首引起的部分倒装 → 强调“只有这样”这一唯一有效方式。
